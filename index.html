ML for music and audio

I am currently working as a research assistant on the “Automatic Music Performance Assessment & Feedback” project at the C4DM-QMUL funded by ABRSM. I also worked on the Multi-instrument Automatic Music Transcription (AMT) project at the C4DM, in collaboration with Huawei. Prior to that, I worked as a research scientist at Cochlear.ai for several years. I also worked as a researcher at Seoul National University's Institute for Industrial Systems Innovation, and Music and Audio Research Group (SNU-MARG).

Current Research Interests:

  • Multimodal approach to music performance assessment and feedback
  • A generative music model that interacts with humans through symbolic prompts
  • Multi-task learning approach to low-resource music transcription
  • Industry-scale music retrieval system and RAG
